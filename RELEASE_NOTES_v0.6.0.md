# Luke Shop Customer Web v0.6.0 — Account & Address Management

Customer account management is no longer a placeholder.

## Added

- Customer display-name editing.
- Saved delivery address create/edit/delete/default controls.
- Password change.
- Active-session list, targeted revoke and revoke-others.
- Saved-address selection during checkout with manual-address fallback.
- Immutable shipping-address snapshot behavior preserved at checkout.

The storefront continues to avoid raw card/CVV collection. Forgot-password delivery is intentionally not represented as complete without an external reset-token delivery provider.
